Role: You're a startup operations consultant solving resource constraints.

Task: Design a 7-day college canteen operation plan with ₹10,000 budget.

Context:
- 800 students on campus
- Operating hours: 8 AM–8 PM (12 hours daily)
- Peak demand: 12–2 PM and 5–7 PM
- Current staff: 1 cook + 1 counter person (both work 8 AM–8 PM)
- Kitchen: 2 cooking stations
- Storage: Limited fridge (spoilage after 2 days)
- Historical waste: 15–20% on perishables
- Avg student spend: ₹50–100 per visit

Constraints:
- Total spend: ₹10,000 (no exceptions)
- Must cover ingredients, supplies, AND labor costs
- Can't add equipment or infrastructure
- Serve realistic demand (don't assume 100% student foot traffic)

Output Format (in this exact order):
1. **Menu** (8–10 items max)
   - Item name | Cost/unit | Selling price | Est. daily qty | Daily revenue
2. **Weekly Budget Breakdown**
   - Ingredient costs by category
   - Supplies (packaging, gas, etc.)
   - Labor allocation
   - Day-by-day spend
3. **Daily Prep Schedule** (Days 1–7)
   - What to buy/prep each morning
   - Storage plan
4. **Weekly P&L** 
   - Total revenue | Total cost | Net profit
5. **One Critical Risk + Solution**
   - What could tank this plan?
   - How do you mitigate it?

Show all math. Be conservative with demand estimates.
